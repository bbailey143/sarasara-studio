# Loose, grainy charcoal behavior target

## Source and evidence boundary

The artist supplied an annotated visual target on 2026-08-19 with four labeled regions: **Stamp**, **1 Layer**, **Multi-Layer**, and **Smudge**. The temporary screenshot itself is not shipped in the repository. This document preserves the behavior conclusions needed to continue work.

This is artist reference evidence, not a measurement of particle size, pressure, material recipe, paper height, optical density, or brush geometry. Every normalized value used to approach it remains a stand-in until artist review.

## Required visible relationships

### Stamp / first contact

- Contact is an irregular cluster of grain and broken coverage, not a solid circular disk.
- The outer contact contains sparse particulate capture rather than a uniformly soft airbrush edge.
- The mark must come from transferred material and paper contact; do not add decorative speckles after the fact.

### One layer

- Paper remains visible both around and inside the stroke.
- Density varies along and across the gesture, with a darker directional core and broken grain.
- The stroke remains clearly visible but does not become a flat opaque ribbon.

### Multiple layers

- Repeated passes accumulate real deposited mass and become progressively darker.
- Existing grain remains legible; layering must not simply widen or blur the stroke into a uniform slab.
- Crossing or overlapping passes should reveal which areas received more material.

### Smudge

- Smudging produces a lighter, directional veil derived from existing material.
- Darker source structure remains legible behind or beside the veil.
- Strong pressure may press some charcoal into the paper, but loose dust should still be able to move ahead.

## Artist gate

The artist should compare all four behaviors in one session and rate the result. Approval requires recognizable loose, grainy charcoal without a stampy dab, flat single layer, opaque multi-layer slab, decorative spray, or source-erasing blur.
