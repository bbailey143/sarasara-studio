# Charcoal behavioral prototype tests

The lab should show particles, contact load, surface height/tooth, deposited amount, and optical result separately.

| ID | Test | Observable check | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CH-P-01 | Tooth capture | Light contact deposits on raised substrate regions before valleys. | Not run: prototype pending |
| CH-P-02 | Pressure progression | Increasing pressure progressively increases valley contact rather than scaling a fixed stamp. | Not run: prototype pending |
| CH-P-03 | Fracture population | A brittle source creates distinct coarse and fine particle populations under loading. | Not run: prototype pending |
| CH-P-04 | Dusting | Fine particles detach and settle outside the main contact in a bounded way. | Not run: prototype pending |
| CH-P-05 | Smudge transport | Sliding contact relocates deposited particles; it does not create new pigment from nowhere. | Not run: prototype pending |
| CH-P-06 | Lift | A later lifting gesture removes a bounded portion governed by adhesion, cohesion, and contact. | Not run: prototype pending |
| CH-P-07 | Burnish | Repeated compression changes packing/optics and eventually reduces fresh capture. | Not run: prototype pending |
| CH-P-08 | Failure range | Broken deposition and dusting vary continuously with load, speed, tooth, and history. | Not run: prototype pending |

The prototype must keep a mass ledger for detached, deposited, lifted, and remaining particles.
