# Watercolor behavioral prototype tests

These tests belong in a small diagnostic laboratory, not in the product interface. Each test should expose fields, totals, and a simple visualization of the event.

| ID | Test | Observable check | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WC-P-01 | Damp-paper spread | A marked carrier/pigment packet reaches damp cells farther or sooner than matched dry cells. | Not run: prototype pending |
| WC-P-02 | Carrier/pigment separation | Carrier mass and pigment load remain separately conserved while transport and evaporation occur. | Not run: prototype pending |
| WC-P-03 | Settling contrast | Two particle populations with different density/size produce different settling behavior under the same carrier. | Not run: prototype pending |
| WC-P-04 | Drying edge | A receding mobility boundary changes deposited load or optical density near the edge. | Not run: prototype pending |
| WC-P-05 | Wet crossing | Blue then yellow on an active shared wet field produces a co-located spectral mixture. | Not run: prototype pending |
| WC-P-06 | Clean-water bloom | A water impulse moves active pigment outward while conserving total pigment and retaining a center share. | Not run: prototype pending |
| WC-P-07 | Paper contrast | The same contact on smooth and porous substrates produces different uptake and spread. | Not run: prototype pending |
| WC-P-08 | Failure range | Puddling, overworking, and dryback can emerge by changing inputs, without a watercolor failure toggle. | Not run: prototype pending |

Physics checks must report conservation and parameter values. They do not replace the artist review sheet.
